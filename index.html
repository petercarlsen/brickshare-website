What is BrickShare?

BrickShare is a fractional real estate investment platform that allows multiple investors to collectively own high-quality commercial properties.

Instead of buying an entire property worth ₹50 lakh or more, you can invest a fraction of the value and earn returns proportional to your ownership.

We focus on leased commercial assets such as retail stores, pharmacies, offices, and warehouses that generate stable monthly rental income.

How It Works

1. Property Sourcing & Due Diligence

We identify high-potential commercial properties in prime locations with existing tenants. Each deal is evaluated based on rental yield, tenant quality, lease duration, and growth potential.

2. Fractional Ownership Structure

The property is divided into investment units. Investors purchase these units and become fractional owners through a structured investment model.

3. Rental Income Distribution

Rental income is collected and distributed to investors periodically after expenses.

4. Exit & Capital Appreciation

Investors benefit from property appreciation and exit through resale or final asset sale.
