Own Commercial Real Estate Fractionally. Earn Passive Income.

BrickShares enables you to invest in high-quality commercial properties through fractional ownership. Earn rental income and benefit from long-term appreciation — without buying an entire property. Offering lower risk than equities and better returns than FDs if invested patiently.

How It Works (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Property Sourcing
We identify high-quality commercial properties with strong rental and capital appreciation potential.
Step 2: Listing
Property is listed on the platform with full complete details for potential buyers.
Step 3: Investor Funding
Investors commit funds in units (minimum ₹10,000).
Step 4: Acquisition via SPV
A legal entity (SPV) is created to own the property.
Step 5: Leasing
Property is leased to tenants generating monthly income.
Step 6: Income Distribution
Rental income is distributed to investors.
Step 7: Appreciation
Property value increases over time.
Step 8: Exit
Investors exit through multiple liquidity options.

Complete Investor Journey (Start to Exit)

Step 1: Property Identification
We source commercial properties with strong rental yield, tenant quality, and appreciation potential. You get offered a varierty of properties and can select any as per your interest.
Step 2: Due Diligence
We verify legal documents, ownership, rental agreements, and market valuation before listing. You don't need to run around courts for paperwork and legal formalities, everything will be handled from our end.
Step 3: Listing on Platform
The property is listed with full details including rent, valuation, tenant profile, proper documentation and expected returns.
Step 4: Investor Participation
Investors purchase in units (minimum ₹10,000) based on property valuation.
Step 5: Funding Completion
Once full funding is achieved, the deal is closed and acquisition of the property begins.
Step 6: SPV Formation
A Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) is created which legally owns the property.
Step 7: Ownership Allocation
Investors receive ownership in the SPV proportional to their investment.
Step 8: Leasing & Income
Property is leased and rental income is generated monthly.
Step 9: Income Distribution
Rental income is distributed to investors after expenses.
Step 10: Monitoring & Management
We manage tenants, maintenance, legal compliance, and reporting. You can sit anywhere comfortably and invest.
Step 11: Appreciation
Over time, the property value increases due to market growth and rent escalation (varying from 5-7% annually).
Step 12: Exit
Investors exit via secondary sale, voting, or final property sale.

Liquidity & Exit Strategy

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this investment work?
You invest in units of a property through a Shared Ownership Structure (SPV). Your returns come from rental income and appreciation.
Is this legally safe?
The structure is designed through SPVs. However, regulations may apply and compliance is required.
What are the risks?
Tenant vacancy, market fluctuations, liquidity challenges, and property value changes (rarely to occur).
How do I exit?
You can exit via secondary market, investor voting, or property sale.
Are returns guaranteed?
No. Returns depend on market conditions and property performance.
Who manages the property?
BrickShares handles all operations including leasing, maintenance, and compliance.
Minimum investment?
₹10,000 depending on the total property valuation during acquisition.
What happens if tenant leaves?
We find a new tenant, but there may be temporary income disruption. But there will be a two-month security lease if that happens.
Can I sell anytime?
After lock-in period, you can sell via secondary market (subject to demand), vote for the sale of property or wait till the property gets sold off.
How do I receive income?
Rental income is distributed monthly to your account.
