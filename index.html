What is BrickShare?

BrickShare is a fractional real estate platform that allows multiple investors to collectively own commercial properties. Instead of investing ₹50 lakh alone, you can invest a smaller amount and still earn proportional returns.

Your Complete Investment Journey

Step 1: Browse properties with full details (rent, tenant, returns).
Step 2: Choose how much you want to invest. Each unit = fractional ownership.
Step 3: Funds are collected securely. Property is bought only after full funding.
Step 4: Property is purchased via SPV. You legally own shares in the SPV.
Step 5: Rent is collected and distributed to investors regularly.
Step 6: We manage tenants, maintenance, and legal work.
Step 7: Exit through resale or final property sale with capital appreciation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How exactly do I make money?

You earn through rental income (paid periodically) and capital appreciation when the property value increases and is sold.

Do I legally own the property?

You own shares in the SPV that owns the property. Your ownership is proportional to your investment.

What happens if the tenant leaves?

There may be a temporary loss of rental income. We work to find a new tenant quickly.

Can I exit anytime?

Real estate is not highly liquid. Exit options include selling to another investor or waiting for final sale.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Returns depend on market conditions, tenant performance, and property value changes.

How is rent distributed?

Rent is collected and distributed monthly or quarterly after expenses.

What taxes apply?

Rental income is taxed as per your income slab. Capital gains apply on exit.

Who manages the property?

We handle tenant management, maintenance, and legal compliance.

What is the ideal holding period?

5–7 years for optimal returns.
